The Bridge Room

Privacy policy

Updated October 12, 2026

This notice describes the native The Bridge Room app for iPhone and iPad. It covers the app’s local storage, nearby multiplayer and sharing features.

Solo play and accounts

Solo games and bot analysis run on your device. You do not need an account, and the app has no central game server. The current app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not automatically upload your deals or gameplay to the developer.

Information stored on your device

  • Saved deals and preferences: The solo deal library and settings are stored locally so they remain available after you reopen the app. Saved deals include the cards, deal name and other deal settings you choose.
  • Your player name: The nearby name you enter is saved for future visits. You can change it on the nearby welcome screen; clearing the field removes the saved name.
  • Connection information: The app creates a random local player identifier and keeps a small cache of table and seat credentials in the device’s Keychain to reconnect to a reserved seat. Up to eight seat credentials are retained, with older entries replaced as new ones are saved.
  • Current games: Active hands, undo history and session scores are held in memory. They are not saved as a recoverable game after the table closes or the app is terminated.

The app does not provide automatic cloud sync for the deal library. Device or system backups are managed separately by iOS and your settings.

Nearby multiplayer

With Local Network permission and Wi-Fi enabled, nearby devices discover and connect directly to a host. The host’s chosen name and available seat identifiers are advertised so nearby players can find the table. Use a nickname if you do not want to display your real name.

Joining a table exchanges your player name, connection identity and game actions with the host. The host holds the full deal and sends each guest the game information that their seat is permitted to see. This includes player names, the auction, played cards, scores, their own hand and exposed hands permitted by the game rules. All four original hands are revealed to the table after a board finishes. Nearby connections are encrypted.

Seat PINs are for casual games with people you trust. Give each PIN only to its intended player. The app does not prevent participants from taking screenshots or otherwise recording information they can see. Nearby play does not send the game through a developer-operated server.

Sharing and importing

When you choose to share a seat PIN, deal or library, the app opens the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination and recipient. A deal file includes all four hands, deal settings and saved metadata, such as its name. A library export includes the saved deals in that library. Share only information you intend to give to the recipient.

Files you import are read to add deals to your local library. Copies you export or send remain wherever you or the recipient saves them. The privacy practices of Messages, Files, email or another selected service apply to that service.

Support email and external services

If you email support, your email address and anything you include are sent through your email provider and received at the support address. Include only the details needed to explain the issue. Contact artful.skates_4j@icloud.com about support correspondence or privacy questions.

App Store and TestFlight services, system backups and external websites are provided separately by Apple or their respective operators. Their own privacy practices apply. Opening an external reference link leaves the bundled game content.

Your choices

You can edit or clear your nearby name, remove saved deals from the library, and choose whether to export or share files. You can disable Local Network access for The Bridge Room in iOS Settings; solo play remains available. Export a library backup before deleting local data you want to keep.

Removing the app removes its ordinary local app data, but iOS Keychain items can persist separately. Reconnect credentials are device-only and are not a cloud account or a saved game. This app currently has no separate button to erase the Keychain credential cache. Contact support with questions about local data or credentials.

This support website

These public support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address and request details, when you visit this site. GitHub’s handling of that information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website does not use analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms.

Changes to this notice

This notice may change as the app’s features or data handling change. The date above identifies the current version.
